Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Liem Barber Shop app ("the app") collects, how we use it, and your choices. The app is operated by Liem Barber Shop, [SHOP ADDRESS], Philippines ("we", "us").

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile name and photo.
  • Profile details you provide: phone number, and optionally your date of birth, gender, profile photo and notes such as a preferred barber.
  • Bookings and payments: your appointments (service, barber, date and time, status) and records of payments made at the shop (amount, method, tip). We do not collect or store card numbers.
  • AI assistant messages: messages you send to the in-app assistant and its replies, so you can see your chat history.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To book, manage and remind you of appointments, and to let our barbers prepare for your visit.
  • To keep business records of services and payments.
  • To answer your questions through the AI assistant.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app:

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign-in (servers in Singapore).
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google". Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us when you choose it.
  • Google Gemini generates the AI assistant's replies. Messages you send to the assistant, together with the shop's service and barber list, are sent to Google to produce a reply.

Who can see your information

Shop administrators can see customer profiles, bookings and payment records. A barber can see the name, phone number and booking details of customers booked with them. Other customers cannot see your information. Your AI assistant conversations are visible only to you.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Payment records may be kept after account deletion, without your name attached, for accounting purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app under Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → "Delete my account". This permanently removes your profile, appointments, reviews and AI chat history.
  • Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is restricted so each user can only reach the information their role allows.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 to use on their own. A parent or guardian should book on their behalf.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
